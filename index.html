LOAN & INTEREST CALCULATOR

Work out the payment, then see where every pound of it actually goes. The schedule is the interesting part — early payments are almost all interest, and the tool shows exactly when that flips.

The real value is what the final amount would buy at today's prices. It is the number that matters, and it is always a lot smaller than the headline figure.

Interest is compounded once per payment at the nominal annual rate divided by the number of payments a year, which is how a repayment mortgage and most consumer loans work. A 0% rate is handled separately — the usual payment formula divides by zero there. Rounding is applied once at the end of each period, and the final payment is trimmed so the balance lands exactly on zero rather than a penny either side.
